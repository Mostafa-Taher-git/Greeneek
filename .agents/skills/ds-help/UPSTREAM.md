# Upstream provenance

Greeneek-specific constraints are appended after the body in two sections: a shared "Greeneek working contract" and, where the upstream workflow conflicts with this repository's rules, a "Greeneek-specific override". `ds-watch-pr/scripts/watch_pr.py` is the unmodified upstream watcher.
